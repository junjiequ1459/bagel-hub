About the Air Quality Index

The US AQI combines several pollutants into one health-focused score. A higher number means a greater chance of health effects, especially for children, older adults, and people with heart or lung conditions.

About the measurement

Relative humidity

Relative humidity is the amount of moisture in the air compared with how much the air can hold at its current temperature. Near 100%, dew or fog may form.

About the measurement

Dew point

Dew point is the temperature the air must cool to before moisture condenses. A higher dew point feels more humid. It is not precipitation, though moist air can help rain develop.
